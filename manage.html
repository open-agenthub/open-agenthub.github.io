Manage your subscription

Opens the Stripe billing portal, where you can change seats or payment details, download invoices, and cancel.

Your license key

The key identifies your subscription, so it stays in your browser — it is sent only to the license service to open the portal.

Lost the key?

Admins can open the portal straight from a licensed instance under Settings → License. Otherwise we can email the portal link to the address that was used at checkout.
